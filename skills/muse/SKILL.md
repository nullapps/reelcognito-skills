# ReelCognito for Muse

Use the text between the COPY headings of the [reviewed combined v5 instructions](reelcognito-agent-setup-combined-v5.md) as the customer's setup instructions after they approve installation. It includes the complete interview and compatibility rules. The [main prompt](reelcognito-agent-setup-prompt-v5.md) and [compatibility companion](reelcognito-agent-patches-setup-v5.md) provide the same two blocks individually. Incorporate the instructions once.

Use the skill or settings capability actually available in the customer's Muse session. Describe any write before installation and verify the saved instructions by reading them back. If persistent installation is unavailable, apply the verified instructions in the current private conversation and explain how to supply them next time. Confirm preferences before saving them.

Check that a supported secure credential connection is available before the customer generates a one-time token. Help the customer sign in to [Agent Connections](https://app.reelcognito.com/settings/tokens) and follow the reviewed one-time-token and secure connection instructions. Stop credential setup if a supported secure store is unavailable. Complete the interview and ask the customer to confirm the summary.

Preflight the [live compatibility manifest](https://api.reelcognito.com/agent-patches.yaml) anonymously under the combined document's scope, freshness and integrity rules. The [bundled YAML](reelcognito-agent-patches-v5.yaml) records the release snapshot and cannot serve as a cached live response. Report the result and any setup still needed. End setup without buying, entering payment information or registering a ticket.

For a later movie night, follow the reviewed rules for spoiler-safe choices, specific purchase approval, changed routes, and registration of a purchased ticket. The customer can find help at the [Support Center](https://reelcognito.zohodesk.com/).
